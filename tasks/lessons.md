- 2026-07-21 — `pkill -f <pattern>` matches the running shell command itself (the pattern text is in its own argv) and sends SIGTERM to the scripts process group (exit 144, cleanup lines skipped). Use `pkill -x <exact-proc-name>` for a known binary; never pattern-match on a string that also appears in your command. Verify a kill with `pgrep -x`, and dont trust `pgrep -af <pat>` output that includes your own invocation.
- 2026-07-30 — `pgrep -x`/`pkill -x` silently match nothing when the name exceeds 15 chars (kernel comm truncation, e.g. `gnome-text-editor` → `gnome-text-edit`). Use the truncated name with `-x`, and beware `-f`: the pattern may appear in your own shell's argv.
